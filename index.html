CookPilot

1st place · ICON x Lyra 2026

An iOS app for Ray-Ban Meta glasses. Look at your fridge, and it tells you what you can actually cook tonight — filtered against allergies you set once, then read into your ear step by step so you never touch the phone with wet hands.

Partway through, we found that every layer of this idea already shipped somewhere — eight apps do fridge-photo-to-recipe, and Meta's own assistant reads recipe steps aloud. Deciding what was still defensible mattered more than anything we built.

Visual Eyes

Apple Foundation Program · 2026

Wellness apps ask how you feel and offer you a beach. This one asks who you want to become, then builds a five-room museum of that future — four rooms of its quiet cost, one of its summit — and makes you walk through it while a documentary voice narrates.

At the exit it asks one question and gives the decision back to you: which part of this world genuinely felt like you?
