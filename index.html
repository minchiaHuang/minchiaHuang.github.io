// How I work

From idea to shipped

Learned the hard way, mostly on CookPilot.

01 Find what is actually defensible. Partway through CookPilot we found that every layer of the idea had already shipped somewhere — eight apps do fridge-photo-to-recipe, and Meta's own assistant reads steps aloud. Deciding what was still ours mattered more than anything we built.
02 Prototype the risky part first, before anything that is merely fiddly.
03 Test it on someone with no context. Ninety seconds, no explaining from me. This is the part film school taught me, and it is the part most projects skip.
04 Ship it, then cut whatever nobody used.
